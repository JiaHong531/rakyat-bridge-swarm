[POLICY_DOCUMENT_REF: JKM/BWE/2026-V2]
Pursuant to the Social Welfare Act, Section 4(a), financial disbursement of RM500 per mensem (Bantuan Warga Emas) is strictly allocated to citizens aged 60 chronological years and above. Applicants must exhibit severe mobility impairment or be completely bedridden, subject to rigorous medical verification by a certified practitioner from a government medical facility. Furthermore, the applicant's household cumulative income must not exceed the current Pendapatan Garis Kemiskinan (PGK) Purata Miskin Isi Rumah Semasa. Disbursement will be halted immediately upon the applicant's admittance to any state-funded care facility (RSK/RSMT).

[POLICY_DOCUMENT_REF: MOH/PEKA/2026-V1]
Under the Skim Peduli Kesihatan untuk Kumpulan B40 (PeKa B40), individuals aged 40 and above who are registered recipients of Sumbangan Tunai Rahmah (STR) are automatically eligible for complimentary comprehensive health screenings. This encompasses blood profiling, diabetes screening, and cholesterol diagnostics. Should subsequent intervention be required, beneficiaries may apply for a maximum health aid equipment subsidy of RM20,000, which includes intraocular lenses, hearing aids, and mobility assistance devices such as motorized wheelchairs, strictly claimable via participating Klinik Kesihatan (KK).

[POLICY_DOCUMENT_REF: JKM/OKU/2026-V3]
The Orang Kurang Upaya (OKU) Assistance Programme under Jabatan Kebajikan Masyarakat provides monthly financial aid of RM300 to registered OKU individuals who are unable to engage in gainful employment due to their disability. Eligibility requires valid OKU card registration under the MyOKU system, household income below RM2,000 per month, and Malaysian citizenship. Additionally, OKU individuals are entitled to a 50% discount on public transportation fares including LRT, MRT, KTM Komuter, and Rapid Bus services nationwide. OKU children below 18 years of age are eligible for special education assistance under the Kementerian Pendidikan Malaysia.

[POLICY_DOCUMENT_REF: KUSKOP/STR/2026-V1]
Sumbangan Tunai Rahmah (STR) is the primary cash transfer programme administered by Kumpulan Wang Amanah Negara (KWAN) targeting B40 household income group. Eligible recipients include Malaysian citizens with household income not exceeding RM5,000 per month, or single individuals with income below RM2,500 per month. STR payment schedule is quarterly, disbursed in March, June, September, and December annually. Households with children below 18 years receive an additional RM100 per child supplement. STR recipients are automatically enrolled in PeKa B40 health screening programme and eligible for MyKasih grocery assistance at participating retailers.

[POLICY_DOCUMENT_REF: MOH/FREEMEDIC/2026-V1]
Under the Dasar Kesihatan Negara, all Malaysian citizens classified under the B40 income group are entitled to free outpatient treatment at government Klinik Kesihatan (KK) and Hospital Kerajaan facilities. This includes consultation fees, basic diagnostic tests, and generic medications dispensed by the pharmacy. Specialist referrals from Klinik Kesihatan to Hospital Kerajaan are processed at no cost to the patient. Warga emas aged 60 and above receive priority queuing at all government health facilities. Chronic disease management including diabetes, hypertension, and cardiovascular conditions are covered under the Program Pengurusan Penyakit Kronik (PPPC) at zero cost for B40 recipients.

[POLICY_DOCUMENT_REF: KPM/BKAP/2026-V2]
Bantuan Khas Awal Persekolahan (BKAP) provides a one-time annual payment of RM150 per child to eligible Malaysian households to assist with back-to-school expenses including uniforms, stationery, and school bags. Eligibility criteria requires the child to be enrolled in a government or government-aided primary or secondary school, and the household must be registered as STR or B40 recipients. Applications are processed through the school principal and submitted to Jabatan Pendidikan Negeri. Children from Orang Asli communities and Bumiputera Sabah and Sarawak are automatically eligible regardless of household income status.

[POLICY_DOCUMENT_REF: JKM/BAPM/2026-V1]
Bantuan Am Pemulihan (BAPM) is an emergency welfare assistance programme administered by JKM for Malaysian households facing acute financial hardship due to unforeseen circumstances such as natural disasters, death of primary breadwinner, or sudden critical illness. The one-time disbursement ranges from RM500 to RM3,000 depending on severity of hardship as assessed by JKM welfare officers. Applications must be submitted within 90 days of the qualifying event at the nearest JKM district office. Required documentation includes surat akuan, income verification, and supporting evidence of hardship. Processing time is 14 working days from complete submission.

[POLICY_DOCUMENT_REF: KWSP/EPF/2026-V1]
Kumpulan Wang Simpanan Pekerja (KWSP) members aged 55 and above are eligible for full withdrawal of their EPF Account 1 savings upon retirement. Members aged 50 may apply for partial withdrawal of up to 30% from Account 2 for housing, medical, or education purposes. EPF i-Sinar facility allows members facing financial hardship to withdraw a minimum of RM2,000 per month from Account 2, subject to account balance availability. Self-employed individuals and informal sector workers may voluntarily contribute to EPF under the i-Saraan scheme, with the government contributing an additional 15% matching contribution capped at RM250 per year for contributors earning below RM5,000 annually.
